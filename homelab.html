The homelab

A mini-PC in my house running 27 containerised services across 6 Compose stacks, with nothing forwarded to it from the internet.

Everything reachable from outside arrives through a Cloudflare tunnel and lands on an identity provider first. It is far more discipline than a house needs. That is the point — the stakes are low enough to be honest about, and the feedback is immediate when something drifts.

This list is not typed. It is read from the Docker API on the machine itself, because the version of this page that was typed spent three months describing two services that no longer existed.
